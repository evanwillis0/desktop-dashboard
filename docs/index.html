desktop-dashboard

A personal macOS desktop dashboard.

desktop-dashboard is a small personal tool that shows a snapshot of one person's school and developer inboxes on their Mac desktop: upcoming coursework, calendar events, unread email, and GitHub activity.

It runs entirely on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and it is used only by its developer with their own accounts.

Google data

With the owner's permission, the dashboard reads their own Gmail and Google Calendar with read-only access to show unread messages and upcoming events. It never sends, changes, or deletes anything. See the privacy policy for details.
